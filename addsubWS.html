Why addition & subtraction come first

Almost every maths topic your child meets next is one of these two, in disguise.

A child who is fluent — not just accurate, but fast and confident — with addition and subtraction has a head start on everything from times tables to word problems to mental maths under exam pressure.

  • ×

    Multiplication tables

    3 × 4 is genuinely just 4 + 4 + 4. Children who add fluently learn times tables faster because they can verify, not just recite.

  • ÷

    Division

    Sharing 20 sweets among 4 friends is repeated subtraction — take away 4 groups until nothing's left. It only feels natural if subtraction already does.

  • ?

    Word problems

    Most Grade 1–4 word problems fail not because a child can't calculate, but because they can't tell if it's an addition or subtraction problem. We teach both, side by side.

  • ⏱

    Speed under pressure

    Timed tests and mental maths rounds reward fluency, not just correctness. We build both through games, not drills.

How does the split between Grades 1–2 and 3–4 work?

Children work in their age-matched track for the hands-on block (4:20–6:30 PM) so the challenge level is right for them, then everyone joins together for the parent walkthrough and certificates.

My child is already fluent with basic addition — is this still useful?

Yes. The Grade 3–4 track moves into multi-digit regrouping, mental maths speed and the multiplication/division bridge, which stays challenging even for confident children.

Just your child, comfortable clothes and a water bottle. All materials, worksheets and stationery are included in the fee.

Do parents need to stay for the full 3 hours?

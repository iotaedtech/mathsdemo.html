Why place value comes first

Every later maths skill quietly assumes your child already has this.

When a child doesn't yet see a two-digit number as “some tens plus some ones,” they fall back on memorising steps instead of understanding them — and that cracks under pressure.

  • +

    Addition with carrying

    27 + 15 only makes sense once a child knows ten ones can be traded for one ten — the “carry the 1” step, made visible instead of memorised.

  • −

    Subtraction with borrowing

    Taking 8 away from 32 means breaking open a ten. Children who've handled real tens rods understand why, not just how.

  • >

    Comparing & ordering numbers

    Knowing 41 is greater than 39 isn't about counting one by one — it's about reading tens first.

  • ₹

    Money, time & measurement

    Ten-rupee notes, minutes on a clock, centimetres on a ruler — all group in tens the same way numbers do.

Questions parents usually ask

My child finds maths stressful — will this help or add pressure?

The whole session is built around games and physical blocks rather than worksheets or timed tests. Most children experience it as play; the maths understanding follows naturally from that.

What should we bring on the day?

Just your child, comfortable clothes and a water bottle. All blocks, worksheets and stationery are provided as part of the fee.

Is this a one-off workshop or part of a longer course?

This is a standalone 3-hour workshop focused entirely on ones and tens. If your child enjoys it, we'll share details of iOTA's ongoing Grade 1–2 number sense program at the end.

Do parents need to stay for the full 3 hours?

You're welcome to stay throughout, or drop off and return by 6:30 PM for the parent walkthrough segment, which we'd encourage you not to miss.
